# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior to identify meaningful patterns, trends, and insights that can help businesses make data-driven decisions. The analysis covers customer demographics, purchasing behavior, product preferences, spending patterns, and other important business metrics.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end **Data Analytics workflow**, including data exploration, cleaning, analysis, visualization, and dashboard creation.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Understanding customer behavior is essential for businesses to improve customer satisfaction, increase sales, and develop effective marketing strategies.

The objective of this project is to analyze customer shopping data and answer important business questions such as:

* What are the major factors influencing customer purchases?
* Which products and categories are most popular?
* How does customer behavior vary across different demographic groups?
* Which customer segments contribute the most to sales?
* What insights can help improve business and marketing decisions?

## 🔄 Project Workflow

The project follows a complete data analytics process:

1. **Business Understanding** – Defined the business problem and analysis objectives.
2. **Data Collection** – Used the customer shopping behavior dataset.
3. **Data Cleaning** – Checked and prepared the data for analysis.
4. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** – Explored patterns, trends, and relationships in the data.
5. **SQL Analysis** – Performed business-focused queries to answer key questions.
6. **Data Visualization** – Created visualizations to communicate insights.
7. **Dashboard Development** – Built an interactive Power BI dashboard.
8. **Reporting & Presentation** – Documented findings in a report and presentation.
